Datamosh falls, first version

September 21, 2026 · See the piece

Started a waterfall where the rocks and plants are painted in watercolor and hold still, and the water is a datamoshed stream of pixels flowing over and around them. The colors come from a frosted seafoam glass cat with painted pansies. This version feels like about 40% of where I want it to end up.

What I learned:

  • Watercolor in code is one shape, roughened by midpoint displacement, then stacked about 25 times at 5% opacity.
  • The water never sees the painting. It follows a hidden grid of rock and not-rock cells, plus four rules: gravity, look for an open cell diagonally, lose speed on impact, friction.
  • Datamoshing fakes a video bug: blocks from the last frame get copied and nudged along motion vectors, so old pixels smear instead of refreshing.
